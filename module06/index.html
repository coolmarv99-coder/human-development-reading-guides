Maya's story

A presentation is more than a performance

Maya helps at an after-school program where children are preparing short presentations about animals. Eli knows many facts about sea turtles, but his notes are crowded and he loses his place when he practices.

An adult volunteer offers to rewrite the whole presentation. Maya pauses. Finishing it for Eli might make tomorrow easier, but it would remove the planning and practice that can strengthen his developing skills.

Maya wonders what support would make the task manageable while keeping Eli in charge of the thinking.

Reading lens: Look for support that reduces unnecessary demands, uses concrete tools, and preserves the child’s active role.

Concrete visual supports can reduce working-memory demands while the child continues to organize and explain ideas.
