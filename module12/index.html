Maya's story

Change and expertise in the same car ride

Riding home with her mother, Maya notices reading glasses, a missed lunch, work messages, and worry about an older relative.

Then a complicated family problem arises. Maya’s mother quickly identifies the competing needs, remembers what worked before, and creates a calm plan that everyone can accept.

Maya sees evidence that does not fit a simple peak-or-decline story. Sensory change, accumulated stress, knowledge, expertise, and adaptation can occur together.

Reading lens: Avoid using one speeded task, sensory change, or stressful day to summarize the whole adult.

Processing speed and accumulated knowledge can follow different developmental patterns.
