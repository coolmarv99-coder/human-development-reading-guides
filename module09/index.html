Maya’s Story

Feedback can change the room

Maya volunteers at a campus youth night for high-school students. During a small-group activity, one teen shares a career goal and is immediately teased by two friends. The teen laughs at first, then becomes quiet and stops participating.

The group leader asks everyone to name one strength in a classmate’s idea. After hearing two supportive comments, the quieter teen begins talking again. Maya notices how quickly peer feedback can affect confidence, emotional expression, and willingness to explore a possible future.

Maya remembers wanting independence during her own teen years while still needing adults who listened without dismissing her feelings. Adolescents need room to explore—and relationships safe enough to help them recover when exploration feels risky.

As you read: Notice how exploration, commitment, peer feedback, culture, and opportunities to belong shape adolescent identity.
