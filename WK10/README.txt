Accessibility and behavior:
- Native radio buttons, checkboxes, buttons, links, and text area support keyboard use.
- Visible focus, meaningful alt text, strong contrast, words/icons in feedback, no timer, and no drag-and-drop.
- Each ordinary multiple-choice group is shuffled independently with Fisher–Yates whenever the page loads. Correctness uses stable internal values, not displayed letters or positions.
- Student responses remain on the page and are not submitted to Canvas.
